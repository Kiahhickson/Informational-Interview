01Strategy 1 Professional association

Step into public-sector leadership networks

Aim

Build relationships with public-sector leaders outside Queensland Health and learn how Directors and executives approach leadership across government.

  • At least three new professional connections outside HIQ
Action
  1. Join IPAA Queensland.
  2. Attend Leading from the Middle Live and the Irene Longman Oration.
  3. Prepare a short introduction beforehand.
  4. Connect on LinkedIn within 48 hours and invite one person for coffee.
Timeframe

Events on 23 September and 11 November. Follow-ups by 18 November.

Support

IPAA Queensland, John for introductions and my manager for time to attend.

Comment

The plan is working if I leave each event with real conversations rather than just business cards.

02Strategy 2 Industry events + LinkedIn

Rebuild my digital leadership network

My tech-industry network used to grow naturally, and my LinkedIn has not kept pace with my career.

Aim

Reconnect with the technology community and build genuine professional relationships with senior digital leaders I can learn from.

  • LinkedIn profile updated to reflect my current experience and career direction
Action
  1. Update my LinkedIn profile.
  2. Message ten former technology contacts.
  3. Attend two Women in Digital events, including the national awards.
  4. Ask ACS Queensland about mentoring.
Timeframe

LinkedIn and messages by mid-October. Events on 29 October and 6 November.

Support

Women in Digital, ACS Queensland, RMIT career resources and former colleagues.

Comment

Quality matters more than numbers. Success means two or three genuine relationships that could grow into mentoring or sponsorship.

03Strategy 3 Odyssey plan prototype

Continue testing the Army Reserve public affairs pathway

I have already applied, so this stage tests whether the role fits alongside my civilian career.

Aim

Use the next stage of my application to test whether Defence service fits my goals, skills and lifestyle.

  • A realistic understanding of the training and time commitment
  • An informed decision about whether to continue
Action
  1. Complete the next recruitment steps.
  2. Ask a serving or former Public Affairs Officer about training and balancing a civilian career.
  3. Discuss Reserve service with my manager.
  4. Reflect on whether it offers the leadership and service I hoped for.
Timeframe

Conversation by 16 October. Decision by 27 November.

Support

Defence Force Recruiting, a Public Affairs Officer, John, my manager and family.

Comment

Success means a realistic view of the commitment and whether it adds something meaningful, whatever I decide.
