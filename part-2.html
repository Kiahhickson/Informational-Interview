01Reflection

Why my network matters

My professional network has grown across very different stages of my career, from technology and startups to local government, Queensland Health, university and broader Queensland Government work. Mapping it out made me realise that I have actually built a fairly large network, but most of it has happened naturally through working with people on projects rather than through deliberate networking.

The people I know best tend to sit around digital communications, technology, project delivery and public-sector operations. Those relationships have been incredibly valuable, particularly for advice, learning and opportunities. However, they also reflect where I have already been. If I want to move into broader digital, transformation and executive leadership, I need to start building relationships that reflect where I want to go next.

Where my network is strongest
  • Digital communications
  • Technology
  • Project delivery
  • Public-sector operations
What my next stage needs
  • Senior digital and transformation leaders
  • Executive recruiters
  • Public-sector leadership networks
  • People with experience leading teams, managing budgets, governance and organisational change

That means getting to know more senior digital and transformation leaders, people with experience managing teams, budgets and governance, executive recruiters, and people outside my immediate Queensland Health environment.

The exercise also made me think differently about newer professional connections. Not every useful relationship needs to become a close one. People outside my immediate circle can expose me to different perspectives, opportunities and career paths that I may not otherwise see. Going forward, I want to be much more intentional about this by getting back into professional events, joining relevant associations and reconnecting with the technology community I used to be much more active in.

How my network formed

Organically, through working with people on projects.

How I’ll grow it

Intentionally, through professional events, relevant associations and the technology community.
